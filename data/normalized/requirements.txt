[REQ-001] PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: Steady fan current shall be <= 0.08 A at the project operating point. Review the fan current rating; verify with a current measurement after five minutes. The limit is a project choice, not a Noctua specification.
[REQ-002] PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: Active steady fan speed shall be >= 4000 rpm at the project operating point after five minutes. Review the fan speed rating; use V-SPEED, a tachometer test. A nominal datasheet speed is not a hardware pass result.
[REQ-003] PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: The controller shall issue ON when the latest supplied measured-temperature value is > 35 degrees C and OFF at <= 35 degrees C. CFG-THRESHOLD implements this boundary. Verify with injected temperature values at 34, 35 and 36 degrees C. This is a software decision test, not a sensor-accuracy or spin-up-time test.
[REQ-004] PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: The configured sensor supply shall stay within the TMP117 full-temperature-range recommended envelope, 1.8 to 5.5 V. Review P-SENSOR-SUPPLY and CFG-SENSOR-SUPPLY; verify the rail separately.
[REQ-005] PRODUCT REQUIREMENT: The selected switch's published continuous-current rating shall be >= 0.10 A, the fixed project allocation. Review P-DRIVER-CURRENT. This documentary rating check is not a thermal qualification and does not regulate fan current.
